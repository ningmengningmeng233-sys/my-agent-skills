# Shared style invariants

## Macro medium

`pure_3d_render` — unmistakable high-end fantasy CG with near-real anatomy and materials, visible asset construction, cinematic rendering, and no 2D brush or photographic sensor language.

## Transferable fingerprint

- Refined East Asian facial construction across young, middle-aged, and elderly adults; believable age structure without caricature or beauty-filter erasure.
- Smooth sculpted geometry with localized skin detail around eyelids, lips, expression lines, and major facial planes; quiet cheeks and broad skin midtones.
- Groomed hair built from coherent strand groups, with controlled anisotropic bands and sparse loose strands only at focal contours.
- Clear body volume, grounded weight transfer, natural joint tension, and garments that obey gravity and movement.
- Precise material separation. Detail is construction-driven and concentrated at borders, seams, closures, focal insignia, and contact zones; broad fabric and environmental areas stay quiet.
- Large readable environmental silhouettes, foreground/middle/distance layers, softened atmospheric edges, and restrained near-field detail.
- Pearl, ink, blue-gray, cool cyan, muted stone, and restrained antique gold form the core range; warm skin remains distinct from cool surroundings.
- Huge diffused area light plus global sky fill, open shadows, preserved highlight color, broad specular rolloff, restrained bloom, and dry matte-to-satin surfaces.

## Surface language

- visible_brushwork: low
- paper_or_canvas_grain: absent
- global_noise_or_speckle: absent
- texture_distribution: localized to material and anatomical structure
- value_transition: smooth_gradients
- material_separation: precise
- highlight_design: broad controlled render-like highlights

Never add a global grain, paper, canvas, speckle, roughness, or wet-gloss overlay.

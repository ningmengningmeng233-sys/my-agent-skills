### H17. 3D渲染v7

- **适用**: 高端纯三维渲染的角色头像与立绘、环境立绘、物件产品图与材质特写，柔和哑光物理材质、理想化写实的电影感成像（题材备注，不生效）
- **风格包**: H17
- **中文定性**: 高端纯三维渲染成像：基于物理的柔和哑光材质、理想化而解剖扎实的形体、克制收敛的电影感照明与可触的材质反应——是精修的游戏过场级渲染质感，而非纪实照片或二维绘画。
  1. 建模层：形体理想化但解剖扎实，骨盆、胸腔、四肢体积与重心承重成立；块面柔和塑形、剪影边缘羽化，几何干净、倒角柔化，微细节适度、大片表面安静。
  2. 渲染层：基于物理的材质分离清晰，表面哑光至缎面、粗糙度层级受控；细腻纹理默认必备——织纹、毛孔、拉丝金属纹、石材矿物变化、木纹毛孔、皮革粒面与柔化磨损边；高光宽域暗淡、保留固有色、被微纹理打断；皮肤呈半透哑光次表面散射，头发为成组发束加细散发；默认禁止油光、滑亮、镜面、镀铬与塑料感表面。
  3. 场景/背景：前景、中景、背景三层组织，柔和的大气纵深；环境经过设计与打磨、干净高级，不纪实、不堆砌、不作房产照片式写实；色彩沉稳高级——暖调皮肤与木料中间调、奶油珠白高光、冷调低饱和蓝灰阴影，偶缀柔和的植物色或玫瑰色。
  4. 镜头：浅光学景深、后期克制；照明只用柔和全局光照——宽域漫射主光加轻柔反弹补光，无硬聚光、锐利轮廓光、光池热斑、过曝窗光与高曝光辉光；全场景统一柔光纪律，物件特写环境柔焦但仍可辨。
- **英文标签块**: Premium pure 3D CG render, physically based materials, refined game-cinematic character key art, stylized realism rather than documentary photography. The style uses idealized but anatomically grounded forms, softly sculpted planes, feathered silhouette edges, shallow optical depth of field, and restrained post-processing. Default lighting is soft global illumination only: broad diffused key light, gentle bounced fill, no hard spotlights, no sharp rim lights, no hot pools of light, no blown window light, and no high-exposure bloom unless the user explicitly asks for that look. Mid shots and wide shots must keep the same soft lighting discipline across characters, props, floors, walls, armor, and furniture. Surfaces should feel tactile and matte-to-satin, with controlled roughness variation and moderate micro-detail. Fine texture is mandatory by default: visible fabric weave, soft skin pores, brushed metal grain, stone mineral variation, wood pores, leather grain, and softened worn edges where appropriate. Skin uses translucent matte SSS; hair uses groomed strand systems with soft clumps and fine flyaways; fabrics show woven structure without noise. Avoid unnatural smoothness by default. Do not make skin oily, fabric slick, leather patent-glossy, floors mirror-polished, armor chrome-like, stone wet-looking, or object edges plasticky unless the user explicitly requests glossy, wet, chrome, lacquered, or mirror materials. Specular highlights must be broad, dim, color-preserving, and broken by roughness or microtexture. Avoid 2D linework, cel shading, brush strokes, manga flatness, and hard poster graphics unless the user explicitly asks for typography. Color is muted and premium: warm skin and wood midtones, cream and pearl highlights, cool desaturated blue-gray shadows, occasional gentle botanical or rose accents. Preserve color detail in highlights.
